# A6: Confirmed minimal repair and local regression

## Scope and dependency

- Depends on A5 being `passed`.
- Apply only repairs explicitly listed in the A5 repair manifest and supported by evidence and human confirmation.
- Stop after A6 remote readback. Do not execute A7.

## Objective

For every approved repair, record the issue ID, evidence, before state, after state, impact scope, regression command, and rollback method. Run targeted regression for affected years without broad reconstruction.

## Allowed changes

Only files explicitly named in `a5_proposed_repairs.json` may be changed. Every change must be traceable in `a6_repair_manifest.json`.

## Forbidden changes

No bulk reconstruction, assertion deletion, bulk `skip/xfail`, source-material edits, unconfirmed conflict resolution, unrelated refactoring, or scope expansion beyond A5.

## Required outputs and acceptance

- `analysis-index/00_control/a6_repair_manifest.json`
- `analysis-index/00_control/a6_regression_results.json`
- `analysis-index/00_control/a6_stage_report.md`

Each repair must have evidence and before/after state. Targeted regression and affected-year regression must complete. Unaffected years must not change. Formatting, tests, worktree, commit, push, and remote readback must pass.

## Blocked reasons and stop rule

Blocked reasons include `missing_dependency`, `pytest_collection_failure`, `permission`, `network`, `remote_divergence`, and `human_review_required`. Stop after A6 remote readback; do not execute A7.
