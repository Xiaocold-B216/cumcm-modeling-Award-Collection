- Depends on A7 being `passed`.

## Objective

Summarize commits, inputs, outputs, statuses, and residual risks from A1-A7. Create the final recovery point, checkpoint, change list, and remotely verifiable delivery record for the final trusted status of 2015-2025.

Do not repair business issues, rewrite history, merge branches automatically, force push, or upgrade incomplete years. Final files must parse strictly, local and remote bytes must match, the worktree must be clean, and recovery instructions must be complete.

## Blocked reasons and terminal rule

Blocked reasons are `missing_dependency`, `permission`, `network`, `authentication`, `remote_divergence`, and `human_review_required`. A8 is the terminal stage; do not execute any later development stage.
