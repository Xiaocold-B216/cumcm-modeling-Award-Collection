# A5: 2015-2025 status reconciliation and evidence-first repair plan

## Scope and dependency

- Depends on A4 being `passed`.
- A5 reconciles evidence and proposes repairs; it does not apply repairs.
- Stop after A5 remote readback. Do not execute A6.

## Objective

Use remotely verified annual gates, checkpoints, reports, document/page evidence, queues, missing-segment requests, `progress.json`, and A4 results to build a year-by-year trusted status table and an A6 minimal repair list.

## Read-only inputs

Read `progress.json`, `manual_review_queue.jsonl`, `missing_segment_requests.jsonl`, annual gates, checkpoints, reports, document cards, page evidence, A4 outputs, source materials, and business outputs. Do not modify any of these inputs in A5.

## Allowed outputs

- `analysis-index/00_control/a5_status_reconciliation_2015_2025.json`
- `analysis-index/00_control/a5_status_reconciliation_2015_2025.md`
- `analysis-index/00_control/a5_proposed_repairs.json`
- `analysis-index/00_control/a5_human_review_queue.jsonl`
- `analysis-index/00_control/a5_stage_report.md`

## Required conclusions

- Assign every year one of `verified_pass`, `conditional_pass`, `incomplete`, `conflicted`, or `not_verified`.
- Bind each decision to repository-relative paths and list both sides of every conflict.
- Validate every line of `missing_segment_requests.jsonl` against source evidence.
- Distinguish global queues from year-specific queues.
- Never convert `unknown` to `absent`, or file existence to content correctness.
- Produce a machine-readable A6 repair list and a human-review queue.

## Prohibited actions and acceptance

Do not directly change `progress.json`, delete queue rows, forge gates/checkpoints, modify tests or source materials, or upgrade conditional pass to full pass. A5 passes only when all years have evidence-bound reconciliation, proposed repairs are machine-readable, and remote readback succeeds.

## Blocked reasons and stop rule

Blocked reasons are `missing_dependency`, `unreadable_input`, `permission`, `network`, `remote_divergence`, and `human_review_required`. Stop after A5 remote readback; do not execute A6.
